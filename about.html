Architecture That Responds to Place

A practice of 80+ professionals delivering context-sensitive design across residential, civic, hospitality and masterplanning work — spanning 21+ million square feet across India.

Our Journey

From one room to an architectural movement

Founded by Dr. Amey Sheth, ARECON.in brings together architectural expertise, thoughtful design, and a commitment to creating spaces that make a meaningful difference.

With a strong specialization in healthcare architecture, our practice delivers integrated solutions across medical planning, architectural design, project management, and execution, from concept to completion.

With experience spanning India, the UK, East Africa, and the UAE, we design spaces that balance functionality, innovation, and human experience, transforming every project into an opportunity to create lasting value.

Our Philosophy

Design with intent, built with rigor

We believe the most powerful architecture merges functionality with emotional resonance. Spaces that move people. Designs that endure.

Every project begins with understanding — site analysis, climate patterns, cultural context, user needs. We don't impose solutions; we discover them through rigorous thinking and creative exploration.

From concept through construction, we maintain obsessive attention to detail. Technical excellence, sustainable strategies, and beautiful proportions aren't nice-to-haves — they're foundational.

Core Values

Principles that define our work
