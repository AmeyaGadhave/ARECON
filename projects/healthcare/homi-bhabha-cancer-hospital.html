The Project

Homi Bhabha Cancer Hospital & Research Centre is a 150-bed super-specialty cancer hospital spanning approximately 5 lakh sq.ft. in Medicity, Mohali. Conceived as a comprehensive oncology facility, the project integrates advanced treatment, diagnostic, and patient-care infrastructure within a high-performance healthcare environment.

Design Approach

Our scope covered design, coordination, and liaisoning, with a strong focus on translating complex healthcare requirements into a cohesive architectural solution. The planning integrates specialized oncology departments, efficient circulation, and carefully coordinated clinical spaces, creating a facility that balances operational efficiency, patient wellbeing, and the demanding standards of super-specialty cancer care.
