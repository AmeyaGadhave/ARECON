The Project

Christian Hospital Bissamcuttack is a comprehensive multi-department healthcare facility located in Odisha. The facility serves as a major healthcare provider for the region with diverse medical specialties and patient care services.

Design Approach

The design emphasizes patient care, accessibility, and functional efficiency. The facility is designed to accommodate various medical departments while creating a welcoming and supportive environment for patients and visitors.
