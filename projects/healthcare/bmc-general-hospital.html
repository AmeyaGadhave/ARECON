The Project

BMC General Hospital & Staff Quarters is a 9 lakh sq.ft. integrated healthcare development in Chandivali, Kurla, conceived as a major public healthcare infrastructure project for Mumbai’s eastern suburbs. The development brings together a comprehensive hospital facility and dedicated staff housing, creating a cohesive ecosystem for healthcare delivery and staff wellbeing. With a project valuation of ₹46 crore, the scale demands precision, coordination, and robust architectural execution.

Design Approach

Our scope encompasses Healthcare Planning, Architecture, Interior Design, and PMC, with a strong focus on integrating design intent with construction execution. The project incorporates a composite structural system combining RCC and structural steel, enabling efficient structural planning and execution across the large-scale development. Thoughtful departmental zoning, optimized circulation, coordinated services, and carefully considered interiors come together to create a high-performance healthcare environment built for efficiency, durability, and long-term adaptability.
