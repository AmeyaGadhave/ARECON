StatusCompleted
Built-Up Area1.8 lakh sq. ft.
ScopeArchitecture

The Site & Concept

Kalna Sub Divisional & Super Speciality Hospital represents a significant healthcare infrastructure investment for the region. The facility combines traditional sub-divisional hospital services with contemporary super-specialty capabilities, serving a diverse population across multiple districts.

The architectural approach respects the local context while introducing modern healthcare standards and infrastructure that supports both primary and specialized medical interventions.

Design Approach

The hospital design integrates departmental efficiency with patient-centric spaces. The layout ensures clear separation between general and specialized units, with dedicated imaging and surgical centers. Natural ventilation and daylight strategies reduce dependency on mechanical systems while supporting the healing environment.

The architecture accommodates both routine outpatient services and complex surgical procedures, with flexible ward configurations for future expansion and specialization.

StatusCompleted
Built-Up Area2.2 lakh sq. ft.
ScopeArchitecture & Interiors

The Site & Concept

Bongaon Super Speciality Hospital is designed as a comprehensive tertiary care facility serving the northern regions of West Bengal. The facility emphasizes multi-specialty capabilities with advanced diagnostic and therapeutic centers integrated into a cohesive healthcare campus.

The project addresses the region's growing healthcare demands by providing specialized services that previously required travel to metropolitan centers.

Design Approach

The hospital's architectural design centers on specialized medical departments with dedicated infrastructure. Advanced imaging suites, operation theaters, and intensive care units are strategically organized for optimal workflow efficiency and patient safety. The interior design creates distinct ambiances for different functional zones - from diagnostic areas to recovery spaces.

Sustainable design principles include rainwater harvesting, waste management facilities, and energy-efficient systems integrated throughout the facility.

TypologySub Divisional Hospital
StatusCompleted
Built-Up Area1.6 lakh sq. ft.
ScopeArchitecture

The Site & Concept

Basirhat Sub Divisional Hospital serves as a primary healthcare hub for the eastern regions. The facility is designed to provide comprehensive medical services including emergency care, general wards, and diagnostic facilities that address the region's immediate healthcare needs.

The architectural concept emphasizes accessibility and operational efficiency, enabling the hospital to serve a large patient base with limited resources through thoughtful spatial organization.

Design Approach

The design prioritizes functional efficiency and patient flow optimization. Segregated circulation patterns for staff, patients, and support services reduce cross-contamination risks and streamline operations. The facility incorporates flexible ward configurations to adapt to changing healthcare demands.

Material and spatial selections balance durability with maintainability, ensuring the facility can sustain high-volume patient traffic while maintaining clinical standards.

The Site & Concept

Asansol Sub Divisional Hospital is being developed as a vital healthcare facility serving the industrial and residential communities of the region. The project reflects contemporary healthcare design principles while remaining appropriately scaled for its regional context and patient demographics.

LocationSerampore, West Bengal
TypologyWalsh Hospital (Heritage Adaptive Use)
StatusCompleted
Built-Up Area0.9 lakh sq. ft.
ScopeArchitecture & Restoration

The Site & Concept

Serampore Walsh Hospital represents a unique heritage restoration project that honors the region's medical history while serving contemporary healthcare needs. The historic structure, with its rich colonial architectural heritage, has been sensitively adapted to function as a modern healthcare facility.

The project demonstrates how historic buildings can be revitalized to serve essential community functions while preserving their cultural and architectural significance.

Design Approach

The restoration maintains the integrity of the original architectural character while introducing modern clinical infrastructure discreetly integrated within the existing fabric. Historic facades, structural elements, and spatial proportions are preserved, while interior systems meet contemporary healthcare standards.

The adaptive reuse strategy respects the building's historical narrative, creating a space where heritage and healthcare coexist, offering patients treatment in an environment of enduring architectural beauty and community significance.
