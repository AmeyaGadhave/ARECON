Project Overview

Westin Hotel, Powai, Mumbai, represents a premium hospitality development supported by comprehensive surveying and site analysis. Our services focused on accurate site documentation, precise boundary mapping, and detailed infrastructure assessment, providing a reliable foundation for informed planning, design coordination, and the successful development of this luxury hospitality destination.

Key Surveying Services Delivered

  • Comprehensive existing site survey and documentation
  • Preparation of accurate as-built drawings
  • Architectural and structural documentation of existing conditions
  • Detailed measurement and dimensional mapping
  • Identification of existing building elements and site constraints
  • Documentation to support renovation, extension, and upgradation planning
  • Existing services and infrastructure mapping
  • Technical documentation for future design and project decisions
